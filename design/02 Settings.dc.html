VERSION NAMEPLATE · `.VERS` IS THE LAST ITEM OF THE CONTENT, NOT A PINNED OVERLAY
THE RULE · THE NAMEPLATE IS THE FINAL ELEMENT OF THE SETTINGS CONTENT, IN FLOW, AFTER THE LAST CARD. IT RESTS ON THE BOTTOM EDGE WHILE THE CONTENT IS SHORTER THAN THE SCREEN — WHICH IS EVERY FRAME DRAWN HERE — AND IT SCROLLS AWAY WITH THE CONTENT ONCE THE CONTENT IS LONGER. IT MUST NEVER FLOAT ABOVE THE LIST.
WHY THE CSS SAYS `POSITION: ABSOLUTE` ANYWAY · A `.PHONE` IS A FIXED 390×844 BOX WITH `OVERFLOW: HIDDEN` AND CAN NEVER SCROLL, SO A STILL IMAGE HAS NO OTHER WAY TO SHOW A ROW RESTING AT THE FOOT OF A SCREEN. AT THESE CONTENT LENGTHS THE TWO TREATMENTS RENDER IDENTICALLY, SO THE FRAMES ARE CORRECT AS DRAWN — THE ABSOLUTE POSITIONING IS THE CANVAS'S SHORTHAND FOR “LAST, AND AT THE BOTTOM”, AND IS NOT ITSELF THE SPEC.
HOW TO BUILD IT · AN AUTO TOP MARGIN INSIDE A CONTENT BOX THAT IS AT LEAST ONE VIEWPORT TALL. THE AUTO MARGIN TAKES UP THE FREE SPACE OF A SHORT SCREEN AND COLLAPSES TO NOTHING WHEN THERE IS NONE, WHICH IS BOTH HALVES OF THE RULE FROM ONE DECLARATION. KEEP A FIXED MINIMUM GAP ABOVE IT AS WELL (16), FOR THE OVERFLOWING CASE WHERE THE AUTO MARGIN HAS COLLAPSED AND THE NAMEPLATE WOULD OTHERWISE BUTT AGAINST THE LAST CARD.
SPACE BELOW · 24, AS DRAWN, AND ON A DEVICE THE HOME-INDICATOR / GESTURE-BAR INSET SITS UNDER THAT 24 — THE PHONE'S BOTTOM EDGE HERE IS THE PHYSICAL SCREEN EDGE, WHICH THIS CANVAS CANNOT DRAW. NOTHING ELSE ON THIS SCREEN IS PINNED, SO THE INSET BELONGS TO THE SCROLLED CONTENT AND NOT TO THE FRAME: SPENT AS FRAME PADDING IT WOULD SHORTEN THE SCROLL VIEWPORT AND CUT THE LAST ROW OFF AT THE GESTURE BAR INSTEAD OF LETTING IT SCROLL CLEAR OF IT.
HISTORY · SHIPPED FIRST AS AN OVERLAY PINNED OVER THE SCROLL REGION, ALWAYS VISIBLE, WITH THE LIST PADDED TO CLEAR IT. THE PRODUCT OWNER REJECTED IT ON SIGHT (2026-08-19) — A PERMANENT ROW HOVERING OVER MOVING CONTENT READS AS A DEFECT, NOT AS A NAMEPLATE.
